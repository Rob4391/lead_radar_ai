# Agency Lead Finder - Business Plan

## Vision
Build a lead generation platform for Digital Marketing Agencies, SEO Agencies, and Web Development Agencies.

The platform identifies businesses that have a poor online presence and are likely to buy marketing, SEO, website, or automation services.

---

# Problem

Agencies need new clients every month.

Current solutions:
- Apollo
- ZoomInfo
- Lusha
- Clay

Problems:
- Expensive
- Focused on US markets
- Not optimized for Indian local businesses
- Too many features

---

# Core Value Proposition

Find businesses that:
- Have no website
- Have outdated websites
- Have low Google Reviews
- Have weak social media presence
- Do not use WhatsApp Business effectively

---

# Ideal Customer

1. Digital Marketing Agencies
2. SEO Agencies
3. Web Development Agencies
4. Freelance Marketers
5. Lead Generation Agencies

---

# MVP Features (Version 1)

## Search

Input:
- City
- Business Category

Example:
- Ahmedabad
- Dentist

## Lead Results

Output:
- Business Name
- Phone Number
- Website
- Google Review Count
- Instagram URL
- Facebook URL
- Email (if available)

## Opportunity Score

Example:

100 = No Website + Few Reviews

20 = Good Online Presence

## AI Outreach Generator

Generate:
- Cold Email
- LinkedIn Message
- WhatsApp Message

## CSV Export

Export all leads.

---

# Killer Feature

Online Presence Audit

Checks:
- Website Exists?
- Mobile Friendly?
- SSL Installed?
- Reviews Count?
- Instagram Exists?
- Facebook Exists?

Returns a score.

2. Get first agency customer.
3. Build only requested features.
